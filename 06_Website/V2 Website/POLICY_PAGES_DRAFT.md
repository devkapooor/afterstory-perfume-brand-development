## Privacy Policy

AFTERSTORY collects only the information needed to process and support an order: customer name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order details, and payment/order reference information.

This information is used to process payment, prepare and deliver orders, provide customer support, issue invoices, maintain required business records, and prevent fraud or misuse. Payment-card and payment-instrument details are processed by the configured payment provider and are not stored by AFTERSTORY.

Customer information is shared only with service providers needed to complete the order, such as the payment provider and delivery partner, or where required by law. Marketing messages require a separate affirmative opt-in. Customers may contact afterstory@gmail.com to request access, correction, or withdrawal of optional marketing consent, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

Information is retained only for as long as needed for order fulfilment, support, tax/accounting records, dispute handling, and legal compliance.

## Terms of Sale

By placing an order, the customer confirms that the delivery information supplied is accurate and that they are permitted to use the selected payment method. Orders are accepted only after successful payment confirmation. Cash on delivery is not offered.

Product images and descriptions are intended to help customers understand the product. Fragrance perception, skin chemistry, and wear experience vary by person. AFTERSTORY does not make unverified longevity, projection, medical, or performance claims.

An item marked **Under Preparation** is not available to purchase at that time. AFTERSTORY may correct an obvious pricing, stock, or listing error before dispatch and will contact the customer if an affected paid order cannot be fulfilled.
