# AFTERSTORY Fill-Weight Calibration

Use this file to establish scent-specific fill weights for 50 mL and 8 mL bottles. This is the source of truth for measured yield and blend density; it does not replace formula, inventory, or sales records.

## Working Formula

`Target blend weight = target bottle volume in mL x measured finished-blend density in g/mL`

At the locked 25% w/w concentration:

- Oil weight = target blend weight x 0.25
- Premix weight = target blend weight x 0.75

Do not treat a calculated density as locked until all bottles in the batch are confirmed full, residual blend is weighed or confirmed absent, and transfer loss is noted.

## How To Calibrate Each Future Scent

1. Record exact oil and premix weights before mixing.
2. Fill bottles to the intended fill line.
3. Record full 50 mL count, full 8 mL count, and each partial fill.
4. Weigh any residual blend and note any visible transfer loss.
5. Calculate actual liquid mass used, then divide by total filled volume to get blend density.
6. Enter the resulting 50 mL and 8 mL target weights here, and mark them `locked` only after a repeat batch agrees.
