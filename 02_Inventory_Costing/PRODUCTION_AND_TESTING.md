## Fill-Weight Calibration

Use this section to establish scent-specific fill weights for 50 mL and 8 mL bottles. This is the source of truth for measured yield and blend density; it does not replace formula, inventory, or sales records.

### Working Formula

`Target blend weight = target bottle volume in mL x measured finished-blend density in g/mL`

At the locked 25% w/w concentration:

- Oil weight = target blend weight x 0.25
- Premix weight = target blend weight x 0.75

Do not treat a calculated density as locked until all bottles in the batch are confirmed full, residual blend is weighed or confirmed absent, and transfer loss is noted.

### How To Calibrate Each Future Scent

1. Record exact oil and premix weights before mixing.
2. Fill bottles to the intended fill line.
3. Record full 50 mL count, full 8 mL count, and each partial fill.
4. Weigh any residual blend and note any visible transfer loss.
5. Calculate actual liquid mass used, then divide by total filled volume to get blend density.
6. Enter the resulting 50 mL and 8 mL target weights here, and mark them `locked` only after a repeat batch agrees.

## Production Route and Compliance Research

Updated 2026-09-06. Initial research, not exhaustive legal clearance or premises approval.

### Confirmed Setup

Founder intends to blend, fill, label and pack at home personally. GST registration exists; cosmetics manufacturing licence does not.

### Findings and Sources

The Cosmetics Rules 2020 require state licensing for manufacture for sale/distribution (rules 23-26). Seventh Schedule I(A) excludes premises used for residence or interconnected with residential areas. GST does not replace manufacturing permission. Ordinary home production as proposed is therefore not an acceptable commercial route under these provisions.

Official rules: https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/resources/UploadCDSCOWeb/2022/cos_rules/Cosmetics%20Rules%202020.pdf

Reviewed 2025 amendment: https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/resources/UploadCDSCOWeb/2018/UploadGazette_NotificationsFiles/2025.07.29_G.S.R.%20513(E)_Certain%20amendments%20in%20Cosmetics%20Rule%202020.pdf

The amendment changes testing/record provisions but does not remove the residential-premises restriction. Current state implementation still needs confirmation.

### Plan Change

Do not proceed with commercial home production on GST alone. Continue branding concepts, documentation and research. Existing costing and the INR 5000 cash envelope do not include establishing licensed production; final commercial feasibility is unresolved.

Recommended investigation: licensed contract manufacture of finished, labelled AFTERSTORY products. Obtain actual minimum quantities and delivered costs; verify licence scope, testing and labelling responsibilities. Supplier acceptance of founder oils/bottles is not assumed. Outsourcing does not permit borrowing a licence number to manufacture at home.

Alternative: separate compliant premises and own licence, with actual setup/staff/testing costs. A loan licence is a distinct arrangement to assess with the authority, not a universal requirement for every outsourced brand.
