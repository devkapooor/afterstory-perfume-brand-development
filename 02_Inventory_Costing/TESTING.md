## Stock Constraints

At assumed oil density 0.90 g/mL and nominal 25% input volume, a 50 mL bottle needs 11.25 g oil before losses. A 25 g sample therefore supports only about 2 bottles before tests/losses. This applies to Bianco Latte, Devotion and other 25 g oils. No sample-sized oil should be promised as continuously available without a replenishment plan.

At that same nominal ratio, current 3750 mL premix stock supports about 5000 mL total blend before losses, assuming enough suitable oil. Filling all 648 pocket bottles alone would require 5184 mL blend and 3888 mL base; filling all 48 large bottles adds another 1800 mL base. Empty-bottle count is not a production target.
