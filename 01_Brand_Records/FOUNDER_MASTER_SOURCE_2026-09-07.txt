Below is the consolidated AFTERSTORY project master based on what we have finalized so far. You can copy this directly into your project folder as your source of truth.
AFTERSTORY — MASTER PROJECT SPECIFICATION
1. Brand
Brand: AFTERSTORY
Instagram: @wearafterstory
Website: afterstory.in
Established: 2026
Category: Unisex fragrance
Positioning: Accessible mid-premium / niche-inspired fragrance brand
Primary product: Extrait de Parfum
Concentration: 25%
Maceration: 14–21 days
Core brand line
Fragrance for what remains after the moment.
Other established brand language:
Scents for what remains.
Made to be remembered.
Brand concept
AFTERSTORY treats fragrances as chapters attached to memories.
The communication should focus on moments, people, places, absence, presence and what lingers afterward—not conventional ingredient-heavy perfume marketing.

3. NAMING SYSTEM
Every fragrance name must remain exactly two words.
The two-word fragrance name begins its corresponding story sentence.
Example:
DARK WOOD
Dark wood, where warmth settles beneath the silence.

Do not casually rename existing fragrances. The above 13 names are currently locked.
