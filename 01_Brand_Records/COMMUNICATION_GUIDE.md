# AFTERSTORY Communication Guide

## Customer Thank-You WhatsApp

- Warm, concise, and personal; thank the customer for choosing AFTERSTORY.
- Connect the fragrance to remembered moments without overstating product claims.
- Invite personal wear feedback using the brand's "what stays with you" language.
- Do not add delivery, payment, discount, or order-detail claims unless confirmed for that message.

## Customer Order Updates

- Use a warm, concise, personal tone suitable for WhatsApp; address the customer by name when available.
- State the confirmed order status clearly and early, distinguishing readiness for dispatch from actual dispatch.
- Thank the customer for choosing AFTERSTORY and add a brief, natural expression of anticipation for their fragrance experience when appropriate.
- Keep the focus on the update; avoid overstated claims or unnecessary promotional language.
- Include delivery timing, tracking, payment, or other order details only when confirmed for that message.
- Apply these principles automatically to future customer order updates. Ask about revising the standard only when making a meaningful change to the communication style.
- These are reusable style principles, not an exact-message template.
