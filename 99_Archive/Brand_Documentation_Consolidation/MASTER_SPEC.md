## Locked Packaging Architecture

8 mL: 60 x 55 mm continuous wrap, four 15 mm panels. Front: brand, chapter, fragrance name and accent. Story: vertical sentence. Product: Extrait de Parfum / 8 ML / 25% on one vertical axis. Scent: three-part profile. Ivory, charcoal, vertical typography, strong negative space; no gradients or clutter.

50 mL: supplied bottle diameter 45 mm; 138 x 40 mm wrap, approximately 3.4 mm seam gap. Panels 34.5 / 69 / 34.5 mm. Left: notes, website, handle, SCENTS FOR WHAT REMAINS. Middle: brand, chapter, name, fine rule, story, Extrait de Parfum, 25% concentration, separator, 50 ML. Right: scent-specific artwork only. Warm ivory textured appearance, fine charcoal rules, subtly rounded corners. Latest founder height revision supersedes both the 138 x 45 mm wrap and earlier 50 x 50 mm front-label proposal. Existing PDFs require adaptation to 40 mm height.

Detailed scent-specific artwork concepts, Instagram scenes, photography settings, caption rules and six-post narrative sequence remain in the preserved founder source. Follow its locked visual system; individual approval of the twelve non-DARK-WOOD artworks remains TBD.
