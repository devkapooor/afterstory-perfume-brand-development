# AFTERSTORY Brand Principles

Founder instructions, 2026-09-07. Use with MASTER_SPEC.md and the Instagram visual style master.

- Keep the brand independent and understated rather than mainstream or mass-market.
- Prefer quiet, specific, emotionally recognizable moments. Leave space for the viewer to complete the story.
- Show evidence that something just happened. Absence can carry the story without showing people.
- Copy and imagery should complement each other, not repeat the same message.
- Vary the narrative; do not repeat "memories remain" in every post.
- Express premium quality through restraint, whitespace, typography, texture and narrative, not gold or ornamentation.

## Format Distinction

Locked label background and brand-kit ivory: #F6F1E6 (RGB 246, 241, 230), for both formats. Match artwork background edges to this colour when blending into the label. See BRAND_KIT.md.

8 ML is discovery / compact: vertical four-face wrap, minimal information, restrained scent accent, no large illustration, 60 x 55 mm.

50 ML is premium / collectible: horizontal editorial layout, 1:2:1 architecture, scent-specific monochrome artwork, more storytelling and information, 138 x 40 mm. Never enlarge the 8 ML design as a substitute for this system.

## Captions and Images

Build an incident, not a quote: place -> small physical details -> implied person or moment -> unresolved emotional residue.

Reference caption:

> You had already left.
> But your scarf was still on the seat,
> the coffee was still warm,
> and the car still smelled like you.
> I sat there a little longer than I needed to.

Show the aftermath, not necessarily the event. Car: empty passenger seat, scarf, coffee, rain. Cafe: two cups, one empty chair, receipt, late-evening light. Avoid replacing these specific incidents with generic memory quotes.
