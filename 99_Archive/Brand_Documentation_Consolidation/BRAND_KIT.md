## Current 50 ML Typography

Founder confirmed applying these settings to the current 138 x 40 mm label:

| Element | Font | Size | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AFTERSTORY wordmark | Free Serif | 16 pt | Uppercase; generous letter spacing |
| Fragrance name (DARK WOOD) | Free Serif | 13 pt | Uppercase; tighter spacing than wordmark |

Exact tracking values and applied weights have not been independently verified. Other supporting text and story typography remain unchanged. These settings are not an approval of complete print artwork or a change to the 8 ML typography.
